      All communes

      Click a column to sort. Flags explain when the ranking should not be trusted at face value.

      Each stop is assigned to the commune whose centre is nearest, not to the commune it is actually in. Le Mans is large and its centre is in the middle, so stops in its outer neighbourhoods are often counted for the small neighbouring communes — which inflates them and deflates Le Mans.

      How these numbers are built
      • Sources: SETRAM static GTFS (routes, stops, timetable) and GTFS-RT TripUpdate / Alert feeds, via transport.data.gouv.fr; INSEE legal population of the 20 communes.
      • Delay: for each stop-time update in the live feed, real minus scheduled time (negative = early). A route's delay is the average over all its updates in the snapshot; the network figure weights every update equally.
      • On time here means between 1 minute early and 3 minutes late.
      • Supply: number of scheduled stop-visits on a representative weekday (the Tuesday service with the longest validity), summed per commune.
      • Refresh: live data every 15 minutes, static reference and supply view on the 1st of each month — the schedule and population barely change in between.
